# Phase 10 Requirements - Demo video, agent log and outreach

## Goal
A reviewer can see it work in four minutes, and read how the agents were directed and what they got wrong.

## Scope
- Narrated video of the real console, every claim asserted against the screen
- `CLAUDE.md` (the agent brief) and `docs/agent-log.md` (what the agents got wrong, how it was caught)
- README mapped to the job posting; a short application message

## Out of scope
- Real card networks, 3-D Secure, a hosted payment page, and any real PMS or processor
