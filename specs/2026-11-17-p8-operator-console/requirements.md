# Phase 8 Requirements - Operator console

## Goal
Everything above can be driven and seen from one page, including the failures.

## Scope
- Static single page served by the app: booking events, vault, failure switches, ledger with replay, masked operator log, reconciliation, shadow
- Truth counters read from the fake processor and the fake PMS
- Demo controls only when `foliopay.demo-controls=true`

## Out of scope
- Real card networks, 3-D Secure, a hosted payment page, and any real PMS or processor
