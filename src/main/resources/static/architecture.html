How it works, and how it should work in production

A hotel payment goes guest to processor to the PMS folio. Synthetic data; the processor and the PMS are simulators.

The flow what the demo shows: four containers over real HTTP

Step by step

  1. 1
    PMS to bridge: a booking event; the bridge applies the payment rule and creates a payment link.
  2. 2
    Guest to bridge: the card is tokenised the moment it arrives. Only a token comes back.
  3. 3
    Bridge to processor: authorise, then capture, with an idempotency key so a repeat never charges twice.
  4. 4
    Bridge to database: only ciphertext and a wrapped key are stored. The number is never stored, logged or returned.
  5. 5
    Bridge to PMS: the payment is posted to the reservation's folio window. If the PMS is down it waits in a dead-letter queue with the reason, and replays once.
  6. 6
    Bridge to the hotel's systems: signed webhooks, one id per event, never a card number.
  7. Every step carries an idempotency key, and every failure ends posted, or in the dead-letter queue with its reason.

The rules that keep money safe

  • Idempotent: the same key is the same payment, however it is repeated or raced.
  • Never lost: a captured payment is posted, or dead-lettered with a reason.
  • Unknown is not failed: after a timeout the bridge looks for its own post before posting again.
  • The PMS decides the date: the business date moves at night audit, not at midnight.
  • Reconciled: every capture is matched against the processor's settlement file and the folio.
  • Fails closed: no key mode or no gateway configured means it does not start.

One consumerA second client on the same app key, chain and gateway is refused (4409).
Replay from an offsetEvents are kept 7 days; a consumer resumes by sending the last offset it stored, as a string.
No ACK, no dead-letter queueOHIP has neither: the consumer persists offset and uniqueEventId after processing and handles failures itself.

The bridge's own rules (idempotency, a dead-letter queue, decisions from stored facts) are the discipline this consumer would need.
